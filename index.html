Project limitations

  • Labels reflect the source dataset and historical collection context.
  • V1–V28 are anonymised PCA components and are not human-readable business fields.
  • The browser logistic regression is a demonstration baseline, not a validated operational model.
  • Financial scenario outputs depend on user-supplied assumptions.
  • Do not use this prototype to make consequential decisions about real people.
